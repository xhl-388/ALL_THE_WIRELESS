## Receiver
The basic building blocks of the modern superhet receiver consist of 
- an antenna
- a tuned low-noise amplifier (LNA)
- a local oscillator (LO)
- a mixer (the heterodyning part of the receiver)
- an intermediate frequency (IF) filter
- a high-gain IF amplifier
- and a baseband demodulator
